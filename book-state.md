## Terminology Glossary
| Term | Preferred Definition / Academic Context | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Human Resource Management (HRM) | The strategic and operational management of human capital within an organization | Focuses on staffing, development, compensation, and employee relations |
| Doables | Routine, transactional, administrative HR activities | Readily standardized or outsourced |
| Deliverables | Strategic outcomes and human capital capabilities | Source of sustainable competitive advantage |
| Human Resource Planning (HRP) | Systematic forecasting of manpower demand and supply to ensure optimal workforce readiness | Dual horizon: short-term (~2 yrs) and long-term (3-5+ yrs) |
| Job Analysis | Systematic discovery and recording of job requirements and incumbent specifications | Yields Job Description (JD) and Job Specification (JS) |
| Yield Ratio (YR) | The ratio of candidates at one recruitment stage to those advancing to the next | Core metric for the Recruiting Yield Pyramid |
| ADDIE Model | Instructional systems design: Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation, Evaluation | Standard framework for organizational training programs |
| On-the-Job Training (OJT) | Experiential skill acquisition directly in the work context | Common variants: coaching, understudy, job rotation |
| Performance Management System (PMS) | Holistic, continuous cycle of goal setting, alignment, continuous feedback, and development | Broader and more dynamic than traditional annual merit ratings |
| Job Evaluation | Systematic valuation of relative job worth to determine internal wage structures | Evaluates the job itself, not the performance of the jobholder |
| Industrial Relations (IR) | The institutional, economic, and psychological dynamics between labor, management, and the state | Emphasizes collective bargaining, dispute resolution, and industrial democracy |
| Principles of Natural Justice | Procedural fairness mandating no person be condemned unheard (*audi alteram partem*) | Foundational legal rule for domestic inquiries and employee discipline |

## Notation & Formatting Rules
- **Chapter Header**: Includes thematic title, course context, module sequence, and an engaging "Why This Matters" opening.
- **Narrative Style**: Cohesive, fluid academic paragraphs; avoids fragmented bullet dumps; balances conceptual depth with operational clarity.
- **Visual Assets**: Standard Markdown image syntax `![Figure X.Y — Caption](images/figX_Y_name.png)`. Zero raw LaTeX command leaks.
- **Callout Enclosures**: Standard Markdown blockquotes for definitions (`> **Definition X.Y — Name**: ...`), Case Studies, and Industry Voices.
- **Self-Assessment Checkpoints**: `### Check Your Understanding X.Y` with numbered, analytical questions.
- **Structural & Editorial Report**: Placed at the end of each chapter on a clean page break, utilizing the 4-label taxonomy (`[SOURCE CONTENT]`, `[MODEL-ADDED CONTENT]`, `[EDITORIAL RECOMMENDATION]`, `[REQUIRES VERIFICATION]`).
